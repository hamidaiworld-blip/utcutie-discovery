# UTCutie source admission policy

Status: Conservative, zero-cost source policy. This document does not activate discovery or publishing.

## Rule
Discovery, downloading and republication are separate permissions. A publicly playable or technically downloadable video is not automatically eligible for redistribution to @utcutie. Do not bypass DRM, access controls, watermarks, paywalls, or robots/terms restrictions. Attribution alone is not a license.

## Reviewed sites
| Site | Discovery/reference | Automatic download and repost |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Shutterstock cute animals | Visual inspiration only | BLOCKED. Paid license required; watermarked comps cannot be publicly distributed. Even licensed footage must comply with license restrictions. |
| The Dodo | Reference and original-creator lead only | BLOCKED unless permission/license explicitly covers our use and the actual rights holder is identified. |
| National Geographic Kids videos | Reference only | BLOCKED absent express written authorization for reposting. |
| National Geographic Kids amazing animals | Reference only | BLOCKED absent express written authorization for reposting. |
| SomePets cute videos | Reference and original-source lead only | BLOCKED unless provenance and reuse permission are independently verified. |

## Admission gate for any new candidate
- Identify the original rights holder, original URL, and any third-party audio rights.
- Store a verifiable license or direct written permission allowing full-file Telegram channel republication (and any required attribution).
- Ensure the license is free, valid for the channel's use and region, and does not prohibit download, modification, or redistribution.
- Only then apply the existing relevance, 15–180 second duration, <=48 MB, visual-quality and deduplication checks.
- Save source URL, rights holder, permission evidence, license/attribution requirements, and review status alongside each approved candidate.
- Missing or uncertain rights => reject from automated queue; do not substitute a watermark-free download for permission.

## Operational safety
- Do not activate scrapers for these five sites.
- Do not enqueue their videos solely because the file is publicly accessible.
- Keep the publisher disabled pending separate verification of fresh queue, one-item-per-run guard, working Render authentication, and an actual authorized public-channel delivery.
- No paid service or license is to be purchased under the zero-cost constraint.
